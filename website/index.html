Why your club

Your members carry a yardage book.
Their phones carry everything else.

A folded card can’t show the carry to clear that bunker, the slope of the green, or the wind-adjusted yardage to the pin. ClubCaddy puts all of it - branded as your club - in every member’s pocket, and keeps it accurate when your course changes.

How it works

You don’t lift a finger. We map, brand, and ship.

  1. 1

    We map your course

    Fairways, bunkers, greens and 3D terrain, captured through our Google Earth & QGIS pipeline. Send us your tees and a crest - we handle the rest.

  2. 2

    We brand the app

    Your colours, your crest, your course name and tee names. ClubCaddy becomes your club’s app, not a shared directory.

    Your members download

    From the App Store, under your club’s name. Updates, new features and course re-maps land automatically - included, always.

FAQ

What clubs ask before they start.

How long does it take to launch?

Roughly two to four weeks from course data to a live App Store listing. Most of that is mapping and Apple review; branding is fast once we have your crest and colours.

What do we need to provide?

Your club crest and brand colours, your tee names, and either your tee/green coordinates or permission for us to map them. We handle the fairway, bunker and green mapping ourselves.

Is it really our own app?

Yes. It’s branded with your colours and crest, listed under your club’s name, and your members install it as your app - not a shared course directory.

Who owns the course data?

Your club owns your course data. We capture and maintain it for you, and we’ll hand it over if you ever leave.

What if our course changes?

We re-map the affected holes and push an update. Course changes are included in every plan - that’s the point of a subscription.

Can we add features specific to our club?

Gold includes custom feature development. Bronze and Silver clubs can request features, which join the shared roadmap and ship to everyone in an update.

Do members pay to use it?

No. The club subscribes; members download and use it free. It’s a member benefit your club provides.

What’s the contract?

Annual or monthly - your choice. A €299 one-time setup fee applies; it is waived if you pay annually upfront. No lock-in; cancel anytime and the app stays live to the end of the paid period.

What are the premium builds - LiDAR, GSPro, 3D explorer, live tracking?

Bespoke, per-club projects on top of the subscription: drone-captured LiDAR terrain across the whole course, a photo-real playable GSPro simulator build of your course, a browser-based 3D course explorer, and live competition tracking. Each is a major build, scoped and priced on application - see the Premium section.
